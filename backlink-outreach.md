# Backlink Outreach Kit — RenovateCost.com

Context: average Google position 73.1 despite clean technical SEO means the
missing ingredient is external trust signals (backlinks). This is manual
outreach — Claude Code doesn't send anything on your behalf, per its own
guardrails. Copy/paste and personalize before sending.

---

## Target 1 — Home improvement / DIY forums & communities

Good fits: r/HomeImprovement, r/RenovationAdvice, r/HomeOwners (Reddit),
GardenWeb/Houzz forums, local Nextdoor/Facebook renovation groups.

**Rule:** never drop a bare link. Forums (and Google) penalize that. Answer
a real question first, link only when it's genuinely the most helpful
response.

**Template — answering a cost question:**
> Costs vary a lot by state and scope, but as a rough anchor: [specific
> number from your own data, e.g. "a mid-range kitchen remodel runs
> $18K–$40K nationally, more like $25K–$50K in CA/NY"]. I put together a
> free calculator that adjusts for your state and quality level if you want
> a tighter number: renovatecost.com — no signup, just plug in your specifics.

---

## Target 2 — Guest posts on home/real-estate blogs

Good fits: real estate agent blogs, home-staging blogs, local news
"home & garden" sections, smaller renovation contractor blogs (mutual
benefit — they get content, you get a link).

**Pitch email template:**

Subject: Guest post idea for [Blog Name] — renovation cost data your readers would use

> Hi [Name],
>
> I run RenovateCost.com, a free renovation cost calculator with
> state-adjusted pricing across all 50 states. I've been reading
> [Blog Name] and noticed you cover [specific topic, e.g. "home staging
> before resale"] — I think your readers would get real value from a piece
> on [specific angle, e.g. "which renovations actually pay back before you
> sell"].
>
> I'd write it from scratch, tailored to your audience, and it'd link back
> to the specific cost-guide page relevant to the topic (not just my
> homepage). A few pieces I could adapt:
> - Which renovations have the best resale ROI in 2026
> - The real cost of common renovation mistakes (and how to avoid them)
> - How to budget a renovation before calling a single contractor
>
> Happy to send a draft on spec if that's easier. Let me know what's useful.
>
> [Your name]

---

## Target 3 — Local business directories & citations

Low effort, real value for local SEO trust signals even for a national
tool: Google Business Profile (if applicable), Bing Places, Yelp,
Nextdoor Business, BBB. These aren't high-authority backlinks but they
build the kind of trust signal a young domain needs.

---

## Target 4 — HARO / journalist requests (Connectively, formerly HARO)

Search for "renovation," "home improvement," "contractor," "home value"
queries. Respond fast (journalists work on deadlines) with a specific,
quotable stat from your own data, e.g.:

> "The gap between the cheapest and most expensive states for the same
> kitchen remodel is about $25,000 — Mississippi averages $18K, California
> averages $43K for identical work." — [Your name], RenovateCost.com

Include your name, site, and a one-line credential (e.g. "I run a free
renovation cost calculator built on HomeAdvisor/Angi/RSMeans data").

---

## What NOT to do (per site guardrails)

- No paid link schemes, no link farms, no reciprocal-link mass exchanges
- No submitting to low-quality directories just to get a link
- Every guest post must be genuinely useful content, not thin filler with
  a link stapled on — Google's spam policies target exactly this pattern
