Kitchen Renovation

Average Kitchen Remodel Cost by State in 2026

📅 Updated July 2026 ⏱ 8 min read ✍️ RenovateCost Editorial Team

A kitchen remodel is one of the most impactful investments a homeowner can make — and one of the most confusing to budget. Contractor quotes for the same 200 sq ft kitchen can vary by $20,000 or more depending on your state, the materials you choose, and the scope of work. This guide breaks it all down.

National average: A standard kitchen remodel in the US costs between $15,000 and $45,000, with most homeowners spending around $25,000. Budget renovations start at $8,000; high-end remodels can exceed $100,000.

What drives kitchen remodel costs?

Before diving into state-by-state numbers, it's worth understanding the main cost drivers. Labor is typically the largest variable — a licensed contractor in San Francisco earns significantly more per hour than one in rural Alabama. Materials are a secondary driver, though regional supply chain differences add up.

The main factors that affect your total cost are:

  • Labor rates — varies 40–80% between cheapest and most expensive states
  • Kitchen size — most US kitchens range from 100 to 350 sq ft
  • Cabinet choice — the single biggest line item, ranging from $3,000 (stock) to $30,000+ (custom)
  • Countertop material — laminate at $20/sq ft vs. quartz at $80–$150/sq ft
  • Scope — cosmetic refresh vs. full gut-and-rebuild
  • Permits — required if moving plumbing or electrical, adding $500–$2,000

Cost by renovation scope

Cosmetic kitchen refresh ($5,000–$15,000)

A cosmetic refresh keeps the existing layout and replaces surface-level elements: cabinet doors and hardware, countertops, backsplash, sink fixtures, lighting, and flooring. No plumbing or electrical work is moved. This is the fastest ROI option and typically doesn't require permits.

Partial kitchen remodel ($15,000–$35,000)

The most popular choice. You replace the cabinets, countertops, appliances, and flooring while keeping plumbing and electrical in place. Most homeowners get a dramatically refreshed kitchen for a reasonable budget. This is where you'll get the best return on resale value.

Full kitchen gut renovation ($35,000–$100,000+)

Everything goes — walls, plumbing, electrical, layout. This is chosen when you want to change the kitchen's footprint, add an island, or fix structural issues. Requires permits in all states and takes 6–12 weeks. Adds the most value but also carries the most risk of scope creep.

How to save money on your kitchen remodel

  • Get at least 3 quotes from licensed local contractors before committing
  • Consider semi-custom cabinets instead of fully custom — 30–40% savings with similar look
  • Keep the sink in the same location to avoid expensive plumbing moves
  • Time your project for winter — contractors often offer lower rates in slower months
  • Supply your own appliances — contractors mark up appliance costs by 15–25%
  • Choose quartz over granite — similar look, easier maintenance, often cheaper

Frequently asked questions

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

A cosmetic refresh takes 1–2 weeks. A partial remodel typically runs 3–6 weeks. A full gut renovation takes 8–16 weeks depending on permit timelines and contractor availability.

Does a kitchen remodel add value to my home?

Yes — a mid-range kitchen remodel typically returns 60–80% of its cost at resale according to Remodeling Magazine's annual Cost vs. Value report. In high-demand markets like coastal cities, returns can be higher.

Do I need permits for a kitchen remodel?

Permits are required whenever you move or add plumbing, electrical, or gas lines. Cosmetic work (painting, replacing cabinets in the same location, new countertops) generally does not require permits, but rules vary by city and county.
