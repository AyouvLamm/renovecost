How Much Does a Bathroom Remodel Cost in 2026?

📅 Updated April 2026 ⏱ 7 min read ✍️ RenovateCost Editorial Team

The bathroom is the second most renovated room in American homes, and for good reason — even a modest update dramatically improves daily comfort and home value. But costs vary wildly. A basic half-bath refresh might cost $3,000, while a master bath spa transformation can exceed $50,000.

Quick answer: The average bathroom remodel in the US costs $10,000–$15,000 for a standard full bath. Small bathrooms run $4,000–$8,000. Master bath renovations average $15,000–$35,000.

Costs by bathroom type

Master bath

Double vanity, walk-in shower, soaking tub, premium finishes.

What you're actually paying for

Labor accounts for roughly 40–50% of a bathroom remodel. Tile work and plumbing are the two most expensive labor categories — a licensed plumber charges $75–$150/hour in most US markets.

  • Labor: $3,000–$12,000 depending on scope and location
  • Shower/tub: $800 (prefab) to $8,000 (custom tile walk-in shower)
  • Vanity + sink: $300 (stock) to $4,000 (custom floating vanity)
  • Toilet: $150–$1,200 installed
  • Tile work: $7–$25 per square foot installed
  • Flooring: $3–$18 per square foot depending on material
  • Lighting + exhaust fan: $300–$1,200
  • Permits: $250–$1,500 if moving plumbing

The #1 cost mistake homeowners make

Moving the toilet. Relocating a toilet even just a few feet requires rerouting the drain line, which can add $1,500–$4,000 to your project. The same applies to moving the shower drain. Keeping plumbing fixtures in the same location is the single most effective way to control costs on a bathroom remodel.

Smart ways to stretch your bathroom budget

  • Keep plumbing in place — this alone saves $2,000–$5,000
  • Regrout existing tile instead of replacing it — $400 vs. $3,000+
  • Choose a prefab shower insert over custom tile — saves $2,000–$4,000
  • Resurface the tub instead of replacing it — $400–$800 vs. $2,000+
  • Shop floor model vanities at big box stores — 30–50% less than special order
  • DIY demolition — saves $500–$1,200 in labor if you're comfortable

Does a bathroom remodel increase home value?

Absolutely. A mid-range bathroom remodel returns approximately 60–67% of its cost at resale, while an upscale master bath returns around 55–60%. In competitive housing markets, an outdated bathroom can actually cost you more in sale price than the remodel would have cost.

The best ROI comes from cosmetic updates in dated bathrooms — new vanity, new light fixtures, re-tiled shower, and fresh paint. You don't always need to go all the way to recoup your investment.
