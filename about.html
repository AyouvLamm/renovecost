About RenovateCost.com

A free tool built to help US homeowners understand what their renovation should cost before they ever call a contractor.

Why we built this

Most homeowners walk into contractor conversations with no idea what their project should cost. That information imbalance leads to overpaying, accepting bad bids, or abandoning projects that are actually within budget.

RenovateCost.com gives you a realistic baseline — state-adjusted, scope-specific, and broken down by cost category — so you can have an informed conversation before the first contractor shows up.

How our data works

Our cost estimates are based on:

  • National contractor survey data from HomeAdvisor and Angi
  • RSMeans construction cost database (updated annually)
  • State-level labor cost multipliers from Bureau of Labor Statistics data
  • Regional material cost indices

Estimates are updated annually and represent mid-market pricing. They are intended as a starting benchmark, not a final quote. We always recommend getting 3+ quotes from licensed local contractors.

How the calculator works

Every estimate on this site combines four inputs: room type, square footage, renovation scope, and a quality tier.

  • Scope — cosmetic (paint, fixtures, flooring), partial (key elements like cabinets or vanities), or full (gut renovation with new layout)
  • Quality tier — Budget (basic materials), Standard (mid-range), or Premium (high-end finishes)
  • State adjustment — a labor and material cost multiplier applied to national baseline figures, since a full bathroom gut costs meaningfully more in California than in Arkansas

The calculator runs entirely in your browser — no account, no email capture, no data sent to a server. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Who maintains this site

RenovateCost.com is maintained by the RenovateCost Editorial Team, the same byline you'll see on every cost guide on this site. We review pricing data annually against the sources listed above and correct any figure a reader flags as out of date. This site is supported by display advertising — we do not accept payment from contractors or remodeling companies to influence any cost figure, ranking, or recommendation on this site.
