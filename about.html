About RenovationCost.US

A free tool built to help US homeowners understand what their renovation should cost before they ever call a contractor.

Why we built this

Most homeowners walk into contractor conversations with no idea what their project should cost. That information imbalance leads to overpaying, accepting bad bids, or abandoning projects that are actually within budget.

RenovationCost.US gives you a realistic baseline — state-adjusted, scope-specific, and broken down by cost category — so you can have an informed conversation before the first contractor shows up.

How our data works

Our cost estimates are based on:

  • National contractor survey data from HomeAdvisor and Angi
  • RSMeans construction cost database (updated annually)
  • State-level labor cost multipliers from Bureau of Labor Statistics data
  • Regional material cost indices

Estimates are updated annually and represent mid-market pricing. They are intended as a starting benchmark, not a final quote. We always recommend getting 3+ quotes from licensed local contractors.
